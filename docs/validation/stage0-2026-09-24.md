The validation-only exploratory balanced-error candidate was 0.61. On these historical test clips it would block 9/9 scorable synthetic clips but falsely block 2/9 scorable genuine clips (22.2%); it was **not deployed**. Neither threshold meets the frozen requirement of at least 90% HIGH recall at at most 10% genuine false positives in the four presentation languages. Small denominators make the estimates unstable.

Measured file processing (40 clips): model load 2.41 s, median 1.55 s, p95 3.86 s, 66.83 s total. These exclude upload/network/database and do not establish first-alert latency. A separate warm in-process 4.8 s genuine file took 2.11 s of pipeline work over 24 frames, with five detector calls; its per-frame median was 1.7 ms and maximum 458.6 ms. A 4.0 s synthetic file took 1.49 s wall and 4.38 CPU seconds, with four detector calls; process RSS after model load was 436.6 MiB and observed peak 479.2 MiB. This is a single-process sample, not a minimum hardware specification. The same synthetic clip first reached HIGH at 2.4 s of audio in an offline stream simulation; it is not a capture-to-browser latency distribution. The isolated backend during a sustained stream showed 267% CPU and 778.4 MiB in one `docker stats` snapshot; long-run results are recorded separately when the 10-minute check completes.

The current evaluator supports metadata slices, but this corpus lacks representative per-language, per-generator, per-channel, replay and partial-spoof samples, so those results cannot be reported. The added EER/AUC/precision/F1/coverage figures above were computed from the saved per-clip scores with `scripts.evaluate_audio.metrics` after the inference run; no audio was reprocessed for that arithmetic. A proper frozen validation/test campaign requires the permitted, leakage-checked manifest and a final holdout never used for selection. Stage 1 frozen-head training is justified for investigation by the failed target, but cannot responsibly start without that corpus and generator lineage. Keep the existing detector for the working demo and defer replacement.
