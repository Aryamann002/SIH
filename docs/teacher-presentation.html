The problem and the response

A convincing voice should not be enough
to authorize a sensitive action.

The risk

A synthetic recording can sound persuasive. Poor recordings also make audio assessment unreliable.

The operator needs a clear decision process when evidence is risky or missing.

Our prototype

Assess speech quality and spoof signals, then require verification of the exact recipient and amount.

Block completion when evidence is unsafe, missing, stale, or verification fails.

The audio score is a risk signal. It does not establish identity, intent, or a calibrated probability of fraud.

Next development steps

Make the evidence stronger,
then expand deployment.

  1. Collect consented Indian-language, noisy phone, replay, and diverse synthetic speech.
  2. Use separated source/speaker splits to evaluate and calibrate; fine-tune only if the results justify it.
  3. Add trusted verification delivery, accounts, HTTPS, and explicit audio retention.
  4. Measure concurrency and stream latency before a wider pilot.
